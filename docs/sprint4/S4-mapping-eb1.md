# EB-1 Evidence Mapping

## Legal framework: INA §203(b)(1)(A), 8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)

Petitioner must demonstrate extraordinary ability by satisfying at least 3 of 10 regulatory criteria, then pass a final merits determination showing they are among the small percentage at the very top of their field.

## The ten EB-1 criteria

| Code | Criterion | Letter | Regulatory basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| EB1_A | Receipt of lesser nationally or internationally recognized prizes or awards for excellence | A | 8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)(i) |
| EB1_B | Membership in associations requiring outstanding achievement | B | 8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)(ii) |
| EB1_C | Published material about the applicant in professional publications | C | 8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)(iii) |
| EB1_D | Participation as judge of the work of others | D | 8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)(iv) |
| EB1_E | Original scientific, scholarly, or business-related contributions of major significance | E | 8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)(v) |
| EB1_F | Authorship of scholarly articles | F | 8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)(vi) |
| EB1_G | Display of work at artistic exhibitions or showcases | G | 8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)(vii) |
| EB1_H | Performance in a leading or critical role for distinguished organizations | H | 8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)(viii) |
| EB1_I | High salary relative to others in the field | I | 8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)(ix) |
| EB1_J | Commercial successes in the performing arts | J | 8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)(x) |

## Section affinity routing — EB-1

| Criteria | Primary Section | Secondary Section | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| EB1_A | achievements | impact | Awards and prizes are primary recognition evidence |
| EB1_B | achievements | background | Membership requires standing — maps to recognition |
| EB1_C | expert_opinion | achievements | Published material about applicant is third-party recognition |
| EB1_D | expert_opinion | experience | Judging role reflects peer-recognized expertise |
| EB1_E | achievements | impact | Original contributions of major significance |
| EB1_F | achievements | experience | Scholarly authorship reflects field contributions |
| EB1_G | achievements | impact | Display of work reflects field recognition |
| EB1_H | experience | impact | Critical role in distinguished organization |
| EB1_I | impact | experience | High salary reflects market valuation of expertise |
| EB1_J | impact | achievements | Commercial success reflects field standing |

## Section definitions for EB-1 petition letters

| Section | Code | What it argues | Evidence types |
|---|---|---|---|
| Background | background | Education, credentials, career foundation | Degrees, certifications, early career positions |
| Professional Experience | experience | Critical roles, judging roles, career progression | Employment records, role descriptions, organizational standing |
| Expert Opinion | expert_opinion | Peer and expert endorsement of extraordinary ability | Letters of support, expert opinion letters, published material about applicant |
| Achievements | achievements | Awards, membership, publications, contributions | Award certificates, membership documents, publication records, citation counts |
| Impact | impact | Salary, commercial success, field-level impact | Salary evidence, industry reports, evidence of impact on field |
| Conclusion | conclusion | Final merits determination — top of field | No new evidence — synthesis only |

## Final merits determination

After satisfying at least 3 criteria, the petition must pass a final merits determination showing the applicant is "one of that small percentage who has risen to the very top of the field of endeavor." (8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(2)).

The conclusion section synthesizes all criteria evidence and makes this holistic argument.
