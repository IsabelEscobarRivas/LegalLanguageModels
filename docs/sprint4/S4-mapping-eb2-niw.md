# EB-2 NIW Evidence Mapping

## Legal framework: Matter of Dhanasar, 26 I&N Dec. 884 (AAO 2016)

Three prongs must be satisfied by preponderance of the evidence:

| Prong | Code | Legal Standard |
|---|---|---|
| Prong 1 | EB2_NIW_C1 | The proposed endeavor has both substantial merit and national importance |
| Prong 2 | EB2_NIW_C2 | The applicant is well-positioned to advance the proposed endeavor |
| Prong 3 | EB2_NIW_C3 | On balance it would be beneficial to the United States to waive the job offer and labor certification requirements |

## Section affinity routing — EB-2 NIW

| Criteria | Primary Section | Secondary Section | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| EB2_NIW_C1 | impact | achievements | C1 is argued via national importance of the field and measurable impact of the applicant's work |
| EB2_NIW_C2 | experience | achievements | C2 is argued via track record, accomplishments, expert endorsements, and career progression |
| EB2_NIW_C3 | impact | conclusion | C3 is argued via labor market need, waiver benefit to US, and policy alignment |

## Section definitions for EB-2 NIW petition letters

| Section | Code | What it argues | Evidence types |
|---|---|---|---|
| Background | background | Advanced degree, educational credentials, certifications | Diplomas, transcripts, academic evaluations, certifications |
| Professional Experience | experience | Dhanasar C2 — well-positioned via track record | Resume accomplishments, employer letters, specific project outcomes |
| Expert Opinion | expert_opinion | Third-party endorsement of qualifications and positioning | Expert opinion letters, letters of support from credentialed professionals |
| Achievements | achievements | Recognition, awards, invitations, publications | Awards, speaking invitations, published materials, leadership roles |
| Impact | impact | Dhanasar C1 + C3 — national importance and waiver benefit | Industry reports, policy documents, labor market data, economic evidence |
| Conclusion | conclusion | Synthesizes all three prongs — no new evidence introduced | Coverage summary, section summaries only |

## Prong-to-section argument map

### C1 — Substantial Merit and National Importance
Argued primarily in `impact` section.
Evidence pattern: establish that the FIELD is of national importance using policy reports and industry data, then connect the APPLICANT's specific expertise to that national need.
Secondary evidence in `achievements` when accomplishments demonstrate field-level impact.

### C2 — Well Positioned to Advance the Endeavor
Argued primarily in `experience` section.
Evidence pattern: specific accomplishments with named organizations and measurable outcomes, career progression showing mastery, peer and expert recognition of expertise.
Secondary evidence in `achievements` when formal recognition confirms positioning.

### C3 — Benefit of Waiving Labor Certification
Argued primarily in `impact` section alongside C1.
Evidence pattern: documented labor shortages, regulatory enforcement challenges, unique qualifications not available domestically, economic argument for why waiver serves public interest.
Secondary evidence in `conclusion` as part of final merits synthesis.
